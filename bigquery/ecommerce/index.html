Good to know

The questions
everyone asks

Do I need to know SQL or BigQuery?
No. Your dashboards and your AI pull from the warehouse for you. You connect your sources once, we handle the setup, and from then on you ask questions in plain language or look at the dashboard you already use. You never write a single line of code.
Can I keep all my orders synced every hour?
Yes. After the first historical load, every new order and refund syncs on the schedule you choose, hourly if you want, with no row caps. Your full order history stays in the warehouse, all of it.
Does BigQuery cost a lot to run?
No. BigQuery only charges for what you use, and at a single store's size that's usually a few cents a month, often nothing at all (it sits inside Google's free tier). There's no big fixed fee.
Who owns the data?
You do. BigQuery runs inside your own Google Cloud project. The warehouse and everything in it belongs to you. If you ever stop using Detrics, you keep every row.
How hard is the migration?
It's a destination switch, not a rebuild. Your existing sources stay connected; you point them at BigQuery and our team handles the setup and the full historical load with you. Zero downtime, and nothing to re-create.
What happens when the trial ends?
Your transfers pause automatically, so nothing is charged and nothing breaks. To keep your data flowing, you move to a plan and the syncs resume right where they left off.
How is this different from my current Sheets plan?
BigQuery is a separate destination on its own plan. It's the same data and the same refreshes, just landing in a real database instead of a spreadsheet, so it stops freezing, keeps full history, and feeds your AI.

A spreadsheet is a place to put numbers. It has no opinion about them. Nobody was going to be told that a campaign had stopped investing: someone had to open the right file, on the right day, and notice.

What it cost them

A reactive team loses clients

At ten clients that stack works. At a hundred clients and 300+ ad accounts, it turns into a tax on the whole team.

  • Data scattered across a hundred spreadsheets. No single place to answer "how is this client doing", and no way to compare clients side by side.
  • Google Sheets hit its ceiling: 10 million cells. Once a sheet reaches the limit it stops accepting rows. The team trimmed history and dimensions to make things fit, so they were analyzing half the picture.
  • One change had to be made a hundred times. Adding a metric or fixing a formula meant opening every client's file, one by one.
  • AI had nothing to stand on. With no warehouse, no model could read the marketing data. Asking AI about an account produced invented answers, because it was never connected to the real numbers.
  • The team found out last. A campaign stalling, a budget burning at twice the pace, an investment that stopped overnight: all of it surfaced when the client called to ask, days later.
  • And that is what drove churn. By the time the problem showed up in the monthly report, the client had already lost confidence. Accounts left.

The gap was structural. The old stack had nowhere to run an alarm from, so the team was reactive by design.

The impact

Proactive team, one source of truth, faster analysis

-25%churn

Slack and email alarms fire on the delta between budget and actual investment. The team hears about a stalled campaign or a runaway budget the morning it happens, fixes it, and tells the client before the client asks. Accounts that used to leak away now stay.

2h / weekback per analyst

One centralized data lake holds every client. A change to a metric, a new dimension, a fixed formula: it is done once and it applies everywhere. That gave each analyst back around 30% of the time they spent maintaining reports.

AIon top of the warehouse

With the data in BigQuery, Loymark connected AI agents straight to the warehouse. The answers now come from each account's real numbers. The paid media team asks questions in plain language and gets the analysis and the report back in minutes, instead of queueing work with an analyst.
